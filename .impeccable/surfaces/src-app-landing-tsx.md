# Landing (/)

Scope: public landing for logged-out visitors. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: people the maintainer invited; they arrive knowing nothing about how Stash works.
Job: understand what Stash saves (tools = links, formulas = commands) and that re-finding is one search; then sign up or log in.
Proof: a working demo search over synthetic saved items, labeled as a sample.
Constraints: no sharing claims (feature does not exist); no public/social claims beyond "private"; WCAG 2.2 AA; reduced-motion alternative.
Pain addressed: old page didn't explain and was one empty viewport.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Stash is a cracktro title screen where your query is the one still line and everything you saved drifts behind it; depth is rank. Refuses the glow-blob hero with a single product card.

OWN-WORLD: emissive black void, four dust neutrals ash to ivory, one signal amber for caret/primary/match. One bitmap face (VT323): one body size, one display size, one hero size for the headline. Text rides baselines; no boxes, rules, or cards. Three dot-dust fields drift behind at halving speeds.

STORY: visitor reads "save it now. find it later.", watches a query auto-type into the demo and matches pull to the front of the list, tries their own word, reads three baselines on how it works, hits "> start a stash".

FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark top-left, "log in" top-right. Huge bitmap headline left with amber block caret, one plain sub-line, "> start a stash" primary in amber. The demo query line sits at the bottom of the viewport, items drifting in the depth field behind.

FORM: challenger operate-c-cracktro-scroller-queue (competitive alternate, user-chosen); seed key 93b4b00b. Signature interaction: typing re-ranks items by depth (brightness, letterspacing, stillness).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
